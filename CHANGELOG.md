# Changelog

All notable changes to Glou. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

Glou uses Calendar Versioning (`YYYY.MM[.PATCH]`, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#versioning)).
Container images are published as `latest` (stable, from `main`), `beta` (from `dev`),
and, on `main`, as the matching `vYYYY.MM[.PATCH]` tag. Changes accumulate under
*Unreleased* until the next promotion of `dev` to `main` cuts a numbered release.
Entries start with the pull request that introduced them where there is one.

## Unreleased

### Added
- A wine's drawer now shows its **Movements**, newest first, above its **History**: who added, opened, drank, moved, repriced, corrected, trashed or put back its bottles (or smoked its cigars), when, in which cellar, and the difference ("+6", "−1"). A guest's movement shows the guest's name, a change made offline appears once when it is sent, and a deleted member shows as "Deleted member". Every way of changing the stock is recorded, and the movements are never removed by the audit-log clean-up. The journal starts at this update with one line per wine, "Update: N in the cellar"; earlier changes stay in the History. **Analytics → Added / Deleted / Restored** now count bottles (cigars) from this journal instead of lines from the audit log, so they start again from this update (#848).

### Changed
- **Profile → Sign-in and security** is now one card: username, e-mail, password, two-factor authentication and the trusted device, then the app lock (auto-lock and PIN); active sessions stay in their own card below. "Trust this device" only shows when two-factor authentication is on, says whether this device is trusted and until when, and offers the one button that matches; the server refuses to trust a device when two-factor is off (#766).
- The pencil beside your name no longer changes your **username**: it is now in **Sign-in and security**, labelled as the name you sign in with, and asks for your password. The name others see is the **display name** field ("added by"); the custom app name and slogan moved to **Display preferences**, marked as seen by you only. A refused change now gives its real reason instead of an image upload error, and the profile form no longer opens empty when you come back to it from another page (#769).

### Security
- Changing your username and creating a guest link that lets the guest open, drink or smoke now ask for your password: an open session alone is no longer enough. Read-only links stay one click. A username change is written to the audit log and you receive a security alert, like for an e-mail change (#911).
- Turning two-factor authentication on now revokes devices trusted before, so a device trusted while it was off cannot skip the code (#766).
- A stranger who knows your username or e-mail can no longer keep you out by sending wrong passwords every 15 minutes: signing in from a device you trusted ("Trust this device") still works while your username or e-mail is locked, as long as you type the right password. Wrong passwords typed on that device are limited too, 10 per 15 minutes. When a username or e-mail gets locked, a "Sign-in refused" line with the reason `sign_in_locked` appears in the audit log under that account, once per 15 minutes rather than once per attempt, so the administrator can see the attack (#980).

### Fixed
- Wrong passwords at sign-in are now also limited per account: after 10 wrong passwords for the same username or e-mail within 15 minutes, signing in with it is refused for the rest of those 15 minutes, whatever the network the attempts come from. Before, only each address was limited, and on an instance reached without a reverse proxy anyone could change their apparent address to keep guessing. Signing in again works once the 15 minutes are over, and your open sessions are not affected (#914).
- Guest links made before scoped links were required could still open every cellar and never expire. With this update, every guest link still in force that has no expiry date, or that opens every cellar and expires more than 30 days from now, will expire 30 days after the update. Links that already expire sooner are unchanged. If a guest still needs access after that, make them a new link limited to the cellars or collections they should see (#614).
- A wine's **History** and its **Restore** buttons no longer disappear after 90 days. The audit-log clean-up (Administration → System Configuration → Data) now only deletes the security trail (sign-ins, admin actions, failed requests); the history of every wine and bottle is kept whatever the retention set, and the help page says so. The older bulk edit (tags, cellar, level…) and moving a bottle during an inventory count now show in the history of each bottle they changed (#588).
- A wine's **History** and its **Restore** buttons no longer disappear after 90 days. The audit-log clean-up (Administration → System Configuration → Data) now only deletes the security trail (sign-ins, admin actions, failed requests); the history of every wine and bottle is kept whatever the retention set (without the IP address it was made from, which goes like the rest), and the help page says so. The older bulk edit (tags, cellar, level…) and moving a bottle during an inventory count now show in the history of each bottle they changed (#588).
- Moving, editing or counting a bottle already drunk no longer counts it again as drunk that day, by whoever touched it. Glou now remembers when each bottle or box was finished and by whom, and **Analytics → Consumed** and the personal consumption goal use that date and that member. Bottles finished before this update keep the date they show today (#589).
- When the server cannot be reached and no answer ever comes (a cellar with poor Wi-Fi, a hotel network behind a sign-in page), the app no longer waits forever: opening the app, signing in, the two-factor code, guest links, photo and file uploads, exports and backup downloads now give up after a set delay (15 seconds, one minute for files) and say the server cannot be reached. A proxy error page now gives the same message everywhere (#619).
- Screen readers can now reach the open list of a drop-down menu inside a form window (colour, type, cellar, dosage…) and the actions menu of a line in a wine's drawer (correct the number, edit, move, change the price, trash): both were drawn on screen but hidden from assistive technologies (#729).
- The duration of a backup or clean-up run in Administration is no longer written in a pale grey that was hard to read; all secondary text now reaches the minimum contrast in both themes (#771).
- While the server restarts its database, the app now says "The server is temporarily unavailable. Your data is safe: try again in a moment." instead of "Could not load cellars" (or the same for the wine list, tastings, wishlist, analytics, guest links…), "An error occurred" after an action, or "unexpected error" on the sign-in and two-factor screens. The **Retry** button works as soon as the database is back. Behind this, the cellars, alerts, consumption plan, inventory count, guest links, humidor, label scan, CSV import, analytics and two-factor requests now answer "temporarily unavailable" like the rest of the server, and two-factor and trusted-device requests no longer send back the text of an internal error (#467).
- The wine list, a wine's page and history, the trash, guest links and every page of **Administration** (members, guest links, audit log, maintenance, settings, backups) now also say "The server is temporarily unavailable" while the database restarts, instead of "could not load" or an unexpected error. A failed restore or purge still says what went wrong in plain words, and the technical details stay in the server log (#467).
- Screens now refresh at once after a change to the stock: a bottle changed in a wine's drawer shows in its **History** straight away, with its **Restore** button, and drinking or finishing a bottle (from the drawer, a tasting or the consumption plan) updates the monthly goal, the "To drink" suggestions, the notification bell, the menu badges, the statistics and the budget without switching tabs. The same holds for every other change to the stock: adding, moving, repricing or correcting bottles, the cellar plan, the trash, bulk actions, CSV import, closing an inventory count, buying from the wishlist, and changes sent once the connection is back (#621).
- Collections are now shared by the household, like the bottles: every member sees, opens, filters and fills every collection, the sidebar counts them all, and a collection stays when the member who created it leaves (#476).
- An account deletion now really deletes the member's personal data: wishlist, consumption goals, saved presets, label scans and their photos, password-reset links, and the app name and slogan of their profile. Bottles, tastings, budgets and collections stay for the household, without their name. A member deactivated after asking for deletion is now deleted on schedule too, and the deletion screen and the wiki say what is deleted and what stays (#661).

### Added
- Administration → Member Management: an administrator can **delete a member's account now**, after their password. What the member added to the shared cellar (cellars, wines and bottles, tastings, counts, humidor readings, budgets) is then attributed to the administrator, their collections stay, and the audit log keeps their past actions under their name, with an entry saying who deleted them. An administrator cannot delete their own account there, nor the last administrator (#695).
- Administration → Member Management: an administrator can also **export a member's data** and **schedule the deletion** of a member's account (or cancel it), including for a member who was deactivated and can no longer sign in (#661).

## v2026.11.3 - 2026-11-12

> **Before you update:**
> - **`JWT_SECRET`** shorter than 32 characters: replace it in `.env` *before* pulling the new image (`openssl rand -base64 48`), or the API will not start. Everyone signs in again (#941).
> - **Database:** 11 migrations run at start-up, after the automatic backup `./backups/glou-backup-before-migration-<date>.sql.gz` (going back = restoring it with the previous image, wiki 31).

### Added
- Administrators can now **delete permanently** an entry of the trash (a wine with its bottles, or a single bottle, box or line of cigars) and **empty the trash** of the page they are on (wines and bottles, or cigars), each behind a confirmation that names what goes or gives the count and says it cannot be undone. It works like the automatic clean-up after 7 days: the photos nobody uses any more are deleted from disk, and a wine's tastings go with it. Each deletion is written to the audit log (who, what, how many). Other members keep **Restore** only and are told that the trash empties itself after 7 days. Both actions are unavailable offline and are never queued (#962).
- Maturity references: the sentence explaining that rules apply from top to bottom is now easy to read, at normal size and full contrast (#765).
- CONTRIBUTING now states the data model rules every contributor must keep (one shared inventory, `userId` as an audit field, personal entities, families and groups, counting through `itemUnits`) (#464).
- Administration has a new **Guest links** section listing every member's guest links still in force: who made each, for whom, which cellars or collections it opens, whether the guest can change bottles, whether prices or notes are hidden, and until when. **Revoke** cuts one at once, after a confirmation; the link itself is never shown, and each revocation is recorded in the audit log (#486).
- **Profile → Data & Privacy** now has **Import a file**: the same CSV import as the setup wizard, with its preview, to add the bottles and cigars of a spreadsheet at any time, for example a file exported from another instance, without reopening the wizard (#641).
- A cellar's page has an **Add a bottle here** button (**Add a cigar here** for a humidor) that opens the add form with this cellar already chosen; an empty cellar's plan offers it too (#707).

### Removed
- The unused admin API route `PUT /api/admin/config/notifications` is gone: the email and Gotify cards save their switch through `/config/smtp` and `/config/gotify`. Old "notifications" entries still show in the configuration history (#929).
- The admin "Access mode" setting (direct or behind a reverse proxy) is gone: it was saved and shown back but changed nothing, only feeding a warning in the network check. The "Check configuration" button still checks the public URL and HTTPS in production (#617).
- The "in-app notifications" channel is gone, from Administration → System configuration → Notifications and from My Profile → Notifications: it was a switch that delivered nothing. Notifications go by email or Gotify. The bell at the top of the page is not affected: it still lists peak-maturity alerts and reminders (#671).
- Unused API routes are gone: `GET /api/auth/me` (use `GET /api/user/me`, which the app and the documentation now use), the single-item reads `GET /api/wishlist/items/:id` and `GET /api/wishlist/budget-envelopes/:id`, and `GET /api/inventory/:id/qr` (the app draws QR codes itself, see below) (#626, #625).

- **Move** and **Change the price** apply to part of a line ("How many to move?", "For how many bottles?") and split the line; a line made identical to another one joins it (#471).

### Changed
- **The update transforms your data once** (#471). Each bottle record becomes a line of a wine: records of the same wine (same category, producer, name and vintage, ignoring case and spaces) are put in one wine when their descriptions do not contradict each other (an empty value is not a disagreement), and strictly identical bottles (same cellar, price, size, purchase date, estimated value, and a purchase place and location that do not contradict each other; none opened, none in a cellar-plan slot or flagged "to check") become one line "x N". Before the update no bottle had a purchase date: for bottles already recorded it is the day you added them to Glou, so bottles entered on different days stay on separate lines, and the budget counts each at that day. Photos and notes that differ do not block the grouping: the most recent photo is kept, the others stay viewable, and notes are put end to end with their dates. A wine bought twice keeps one line per purchase date. Nothing is lost: tastings, history, slots, collections and counts follow, and the update checks its totals (units, value, spending) before and after and cancels itself if anything differs.
- What the update does not settle alone is listed under **To check after the update** (a banner with *View* and *Later*, back at your next sign-in): wines of the same name with contradicting descriptions (**Merge?**, choose field by field, or *It's another wine*), and opened or finished records that counted several bottles, which Glou split as best it could ("Before the update: 3 bottles, opened. Glou counted 1 opened + 2 full"; **That's right** or **Correct the number**). A quantity above 1 typed with the old *Increment quantity* button is kept and now counts.
- Cigar records become boxes holding the number of cigars they had left (closed, or opened if the record was opened; an emptied record becomes a finished box). Wines you had marked as known but not owned (the "Reference" option of the add form, which no longer exists: a wine tasted elsewhere is now created from "New tasting") become wines "No longer in the cellar" (no bottle), and the rotating-inventory entries that pointed at them are removed.
- The word "asset" is gone from the whole interface: it says wine, bottle, spirit, cigar or reference, and "Enabled / Disabled" for a member account (#471).
- An opened box of cigars is now worth the share of its cigars left (2 smoked out of a box of 25 at 200 € leave 184 €), in the drawer, the cellar cards and the analytics; spending is unchanged. Each line of a wine shows its full purchase date, the cellar map counts the cigars of a box and tells a closed box from an opened one, a bulk move says how many bottles are already in the chosen cellar (and cannot be confirmed when they all are), the trash heads its sections, says how many bottles, boxes or cigars a line holds and restores them in the plural, and an opening undone at once leaves nothing in the history (#471).
- Restoring a backup made by another version of Glou is now refused with a message that points to the right procedure, instead of leaving a database the running version cannot read (#471).
- A guest can no longer put an opened bottle back to full or edit a wine's notes, which now belong to the wine (#471).

### Changed
- **Action required on Linux:** create the backups folder for the API user before starting the stack: `mkdir -p backups && sudo chown 1001:1001 backups` (README, wiki 01). Without it Docker creates the folder as root and every backup fails (#551, #537).
- `latest` and `beta` images are published only after the end-to-end tests pass (#551, #539). Compose: Ollama pinned to `0.40.6`, web healthcheck, unused `api_logs` volume removed (#551, #540).
- Peak-maturity notifications: one digest per member instead of one message per bottle (#560, #478). The bell counts only what calls for action and opens the bottle (#557, #506).
- Photos and avatars are downscaled in the browser before upload (#559, #494). Signing in again returns to the page you asked for, such as a bottle's QR code (#552, #507). Only the active language's translation file is downloaded (#550, #495).
- Tablets: filters behind a toggle below 1024 px, cards fill the width, the sidebar starts collapsed below 1280 px (#555, #497). Spirits use a glass icon (#548, #496).
- Browser storage keys are prefixed `glou:`; saved preferences are migrated (#565, #139).
- **Action required on existing instances:** `docker-compose.yml`'s `api_backups` named volume was replaced with a host bind mount (`./backups:/app/backups`), so your own backup tooling can see and collect the dumps. Backups made before this change live in the old named volume and won't appear under `./backups` automatically — copy them out with `docker run --rm -v api_backups:/from -v "$PWD/backups":/to alpine cp -a /from/. /to/` (adjust the volume name if your project/compose name differs) before running `docker volume rm api_backups`, or just let the volume sit unused (#92).
- The complete GDPR export now includes wishlist, budgets, goals, sessions, trusted devices, stock counts and configuration history (#283, #224).
- A deactivated account is reported as `INVALID_CREDENTIALS` at login (#373, #352).
- Wiki and READMEs (FR + EN) refreshed: environment variable reference, registration, offline pending changes, keyboard cellar grid, label scan timeout.
- Restoring a backup resets the `public` schema and replays the dump in one transaction.
- Plurals are real i18next plural forms instead of "(s)"; amounts use `Intl` currency formatting; dates and numbers follow the language chosen in the app.
- Page titles and the PWA manifest no longer claim a single language.
- The `PUID`/`PGID` variables were removed from the compose files (the API always runs as `1001:1001`).
- `.dockerignore` for `api/` and `web/`; log rotation on every service of the dev compose.

### Fixed

- Accessibility: keyboard-operable category and level selectors, link-based bottom bar, scrollable sidebar on short screens, numeric keypad for 2FA codes, cards no longer nest buttons, details readable in dark mode (#549, #564, #548; #516, #523, #525, #527, #517, #519).
- iPhone: the + button, bulk bar and undo toast sit above the bottom bar; the cellar map scrolls with a finger (#555; #526, #512).

- An empty bottle no longer counts towards the value of the collection or of its cellar: a bottle recorded as opened and finished holds no remaining value, while still being counted in the inventory and in what was spent (#433).
- First account creation failing on a NAS reached by IP (#247).

- Retention deletes scan photos only after its transaction has committed.
- Restoring a trashed bottle whose grid slot was taken restores it unplaced.
- SMTP connections time out after 15 s and the transporter is reused.
- Several silent failures now report an error (stock update after a tasting, copy-link on HTTP, offline sync identifiers on HTTP).
- Accessibility: announced error banners, labelled and required form fields, keyboard-operable search, wizard progress for screen readers, `Space` no longer scrolls the map list, header hidden when printing.
