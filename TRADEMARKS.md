# Trademark policy

[Version française](TRADEMARKS_FR.md)

The [licence](LICENSE) lets you use Glou's code. It does **not** let you use
Glou's name and visual identity in a way that could make people believe your
software, service or site is Glou or is endorsed by the maintainer. This policy
explains what you may do with them.

## The marks

- the name **Glou**, as the name of a software product or service;
- the Glou **logo and icons** (also protected by copyright);
- the baseline **"Glou - Simply precious" / "Glou - Simplement précieux"**.

They belong to the maintainer of Glou (GitHub: `jackthomasanderson`).

## Allowed without asking

- **Talking about Glou**: articles, reviews, tutorials, talks, videos, social
  media posts and blog posts may name Glou and show screenshots, as long as
  they do not suggest they are official.
- **Running Glou**: an unmodified copy, or a copy you modified for yourself and
  people you share it with privately, may keep the name and logo.
- **Packaging unmodified Glou**: app catalogues for home servers (for example
  Unraid, YunoHost, CasaOS, TrueNAS) may list Glou under its name and logo,
  provided the package installs Glou without changes to its features and links
  to the official repository: <https://github.com/jackthomasanderson/glou-server>.
- **Saying where your work comes from**: a published fork may say it is
  "based on Glou" or "a fork of Glou", in plain text, without the logo.

## Needs written permission

- **Publishing a modified version under the Glou name or logo.** A fork shared
  with the public must use another name, another logo and other icons, and must
  not use a name that could be confused with Glou.
- **Domain names, social media accounts and app store listings** containing
  "Glou", other than the unmodified packages described above.
- **Commercial use of the marks**: products, services, hosting offers, training
  or merchandise using the name, logo or baseline.

## Commercial licensees

A business holding a commercial licence for Glou may say that its offer is
"powered by Glou" and show the logo next to that statement, without presenting
its offer as Glou itself. Its licence agreement may set further rules.

## Using the marks correctly

When you use the marks under this policy: do not alter the logo, do not
combine "Glou" with other words to make a new product name, and do not suggest
a partnership or endorsement that does not exist.
