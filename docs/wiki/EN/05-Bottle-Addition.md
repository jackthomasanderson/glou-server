# Add a Bottle

## TL;DR
Easily categorize and add new bottles to your cellar collection.

## Prerequisites
* Having created at least one **Cellar**.
* Logged into your account.

## Action
1. From the **Bottles** or **Cellars** tab, click **+ Add**.
2. **Step 1 (Common Section)** : Elect the category : Wine, Sparkling or Spirit.
3. Select the destination cellar.
4. Input the name of the product and the producer. You can save with minimal input from here on out.
5. **Step 2 (Specific Options)** : The specific fields adapt themselves to the chosen category (ex: grape varieties for wine, age for spirits).
6. Click on **Save** to add the bottle.

## The "Firewall" (Troubleshooting)

| Error / Behavior | Fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| **The "+" button doesn't appear or is deactivated** | You must first create at least one cellar. Navigate to "Cellars" and add one. |
| **Wrong suggestions (ex: cigars instead of wines)** | Check that the category selected in Step 1 is "Wine" or "Sparkling" and not an unrelated category. |
| **Missing essential fields (appellation, etc.)** | Unfold the optional section with the "Show more" arrow to access the advanced details. |
