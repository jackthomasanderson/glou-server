# User Preferences

## TL;DR
Customize your experience and security.

## Profile Settings
- **Language**: Switch between English / French.
- **View Options**:
  - **Grid**: Visual cards (good for browsing images).
  - **List**: Compact rows (good for inventory).

## Security (2FA)
1. Go to Profile > Security.
2. Enable **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Scan QR code with Authenticator App.
4. Save **Backup Keys** securely.

> [!CAUTION]
> If you lose your device and backup keys, you lose access to your account.
